03 / What to look forShape first, finishing details second

Recognizable initials.

Can you see the R and S without an explanation? The mark should be easy to remember after a quick glance.

Personality that fits.

Joined is fluid and precise. Constructed is firmer and more architectural. Open is softer and more informal.

A useful small version.

The 16px samples expose where strokes may close up. The chosen mark will get a separate small-size refinement before it becomes the favicon.

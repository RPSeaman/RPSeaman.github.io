01 / Identity

A simple signature.

The lowercase name and open monogram remain the signature. Give them room, keep them in one color, and let the work carry the detail.

Space around the mark

Keep a clear margin of at least 20% of the SVG width on every side. For a 40px mark, that is 8px. Keep the name 12px from the mark in navigation.

Small sizes

16 24 38 48

38px in navigation; 24px minimum for standalone interface use. The 16px sample is a favicon check, not the default display size.

Preserve the original

The same letterforms in every application. No stretching, added outlines, shadows, or enclosing badge. The name uses Instrument Sans 500 with a single-storey a, rounded y, and rounded punctuation.

04 / Design rulesThe system behind the appearance

Let the work be visible.

Give real research imagery and software captures room. Pair each image with an honest caption. Use project summaries to explain your contribution.

Build a clear rhythm.

Large statements, compact labels, and generous gaps. Start with a 12-column desktop grid, a 4-column mobile grid, and spacing in multiples of 8.

Keep interaction quiet.

Use underlines, solid fills, and clear focus states. Aim for brief 150–200ms feedback. Motion should help explain a change and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Access isn’t the same as understanding.

An interactive dataset lets readers look around. The design question is how to communicate what matters without losing that freedom to explore.

The need

Help a reader understand a scientific finding without having to reconstruct the author’s analysis or move into a separate presentation tool.

My role

I created the full annotation system within the existing Vitessce framework, with supervision and guidance from Mark S. Keller and Nils Gehlenborg.

Demonstrated in the draft

A 22-frame mouse cortex narrative, a guided kidney pathology example, and an imported 14-step Minerva story show three ways to use the system.
